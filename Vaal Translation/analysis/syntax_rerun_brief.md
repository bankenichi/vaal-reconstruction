# Analyst brief (gloss-blind syntax panel)

You are one independent analyst. Derive grammar from the distribution of forms in the numbered lines you were given. Do not use any other source.

## Isolation rule

- Use only: the numbered corpus file, the form-only token list, and this brief.
- Do not open any other file in the repository. Do not search the web. Do not look up forms in Maya, Nahuatl, Spanish, or any dictionary.
- Derive grammar from this language's own distribution only. Never import the grammar of another language.
- Do not assign English meanings to tokens. You may label form-classes by position and recurrence (for example "recurring clause-initial form X") but not by translation.
- Prefer undetermined over forcing a pattern. Thin evidence stays undetermined.
- No em dashes, middots, or emojis in your sheet.

## Token list

The CSV lists orthographic form keys, attested spellings as they appear, and counts. It has no glosses.

## Questions

Answer all seven. For each of 1-6 give: finding, line-number evidence, confidence (high / medium / low). For 7, list items and why they are undetermined.

1. Clause-type marking. Is clause type marked? If so, by what recurring forms, and in what position (initial, final, other, mixed, undetermined)?
2. Noun-phrase order. Relative order of article-like, possessive-like, and modifier-like forms versus a noun-like form, if you can identify such classes from distribution alone. If you cannot, say undetermined.
3. Possession order. Order of possessed versus possessor, if identifiable. If not, undetermined.
4. Verb-object order. If a verb-like and object-like class can be identified, what is their relative order? If not, undetermined.
5. Copula. Is there an overt copula in equational or juxtaposed clauses, is there none (juxtaposition), or is this undetermined?
6. Affix position. Do bound elements appear as prefixes or proclitics (front), suffixes (back), both, or undetermined?
7. Undetermined items. List forms whose function you cannot determine from distribution.

## Output

Write a markdown sheet with those seven headings, plus a short note naming the packet files you used. Do not score yourself against an external key.
