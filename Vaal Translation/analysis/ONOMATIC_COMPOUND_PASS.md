# Onomastic / compound pass (2026-09-16)

Form-first checklist on C+L / H+L names and title phrases, with the attached Nahuatl, Nawat, and Maya extracts. Goal: a decode artifact that could support Battery-E-style recovery of *Panquetzaliztli*, or an honest miss. No new H. Lookup: `dict_lookup.py` over hashed uploads (never committed).

## 1. Compound checklist

For a veintena name, theonym, or title:

1. Whole-form exact hit in an attached dict (the Battery E recovery bar).
2. If miss: do the pieces exist as free lemmas of the **same** language, with a documented compound pattern, covering every segment (Gate 1 residue rule)?
3. Bound-only stems (the piece occurs only inside other words, with a different sense) are not a free-lemma recovery.
4. A Spanish or Maya false friend on a Nahuatl name is a competitor, not a decode of the name.
5. Do not mint H without both gates under recovery scoring.

**Verdict: missing-compound, not a new etymology.** There is no decode artifact in this kit that would turn Battery E 0/5 into a recovery. Do not mint H from memory of Sahagun / Molina, which are not in the attached set.

The name remains a real Classical Nahuatl veintena (*raising of banners*, Huitzilopochtli's festival). That lore note is unchanged. It is not a protocol-clean H.
